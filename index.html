Body Mass Index (BMI)

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a measurement that estimates whether your weight is in a healthy range for your height, based on your weight and height (kg/m²).

Exercise types we recommend

  • Light cardio — e.g. walking, swimming, light aerobics
  • Strength — e.g. push-ups, pull-ups, weightlifting
  • HIIT — High-Intensity Interval Training: hard work in short bursts, then rest, repeated
  • Beginner — beginner-level pilates, yoga, and similar programs
